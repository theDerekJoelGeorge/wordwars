worsus READY

what this is

a 5-letter word war with sabotage

worsus is a chaotic 5-letter word game. Type a dictionary word in 30 seconds. Letters score like Scrabble. Then a letter from your word can freeze in place, so the next player has to work around it. Spend points in the sabotage shop to freeze extra letters, hide the clock, or force backwards typing.

how to play

  1. Pass the device. The next player taps Ready when they have the phone.
  2. Type any five-letter dictionary word. Letters score like Scrabble.
  3. After you are ready, one or more letters from the last word may freeze in place.
  4. Between turns, open the shop to spend points on sabotages — freeze extra letters, hide the clock, force backwards typing, and more.
  5. The first word scores nothing and starts the chain. Then each player takes the chosen number of scoring turns. A tie goes to sudden death.
  6. Playing alone? Tap Add AI on a rival on the setup screen. Playing apart? Host a five-letter room and have friends type the word.

not wordle. not an imposter game.

Nobody is hiding a secret word. This is not Word Sus, the imposter party game. You chain five-letter words, score them, and sabotage whoever goes next.

questions

What is worsus?

A chaotic 5-letter word game you play in the browser. You chain dictionary words, score letters like Scrabble, and spend points on sabotages.

How do you play worsus?

Each turn you have 30 seconds to type a valid 5-letter word. Letters from the last word can freeze in place. The first word starts the chain and scores nothing. Then each player takes the chosen number of scoring turns. A tie goes to sudden death.

Is worsus like Wordle?

No. Wordle is a solo guess-the-word puzzle. worsus is a multiplayer chain: you invent words, score them, and sabotage whoever goes next. It is also not Word Sus, the imposter party game.

Can I play worsus with friends online?

Yes. Host a five-letter room and send the link. You can also pass one device, or add an AI on the setup screen.

Is worsus free?

Yes. Play in the browser. No download.
