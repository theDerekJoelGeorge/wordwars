about the game

Wordsus is a chaotic word game where every turn is a race to outscore and sabotage your opponents. You have 30 seconds to build a valid word using the letters available to you. Each letter earns points, so clever word choices can give you a serious advantage.

After you submit your word, one of its letters freeze in place, forcing the next player to work around it. As the game progresses, the words become more restricted, turning every round into a tense test of vocabulary, speed, and strategy.

Use the points you earn in the Sabotage Shop to disrupt your opponents. The best players don’t just find high-scoring words, they create problems for their rivals on their way to victory.

Think fast, choose your letters carefully, and/or sabotage your way to victory.

modes of play

This mode is designed for a local co-op play with friends on the same device. If you are playing alone, you’ll be able to add in AI opponents to play against. (fun tip: Try watching all 3 AIs play against each other)

frequently asked questions

What is worsus?

A chaotic word game you play with a group of friends or an Average Individual (AI). You enter in words, score points on the individual letters, and spend those points on sabotages. The one with the highest points at the end of the game walks away with bragging rights.

How do you play worsus?

Each turn you have 30 seconds to type a valid 5-letter word. Letters from the last word can freeze in place. The first word starts the chain and scores nothing. Then each player takes the chosen number of scoring turns. A tie goes to sudden death.

What is PPL ?

PPL stands for Points Per Letter and is a chart showing the points for each letter for that specific game. The PPL shuffles every game making each run unique and make sure you have your eye out on the PPL since players can shuffle it mid game by buying a specific sabotage.

Is worsus like Wordle?

No. Wordle is a solo guess-the-word puzzle. Worsus is a multiplayer word game that takes elements of Wordle and Scrabble and ties it all together with some unique twists. Play a game and you'll see that worsus is nothing like wordle.
It is also not Word Sus, the imposter party game.

Can I play worsus with friends online?

Yes. You can either host a room for upto 6 players or join into a room hosted by your friend. MMO is not in the works as of now.

Is worsus free?

Yes. Play in the browser. No download.
