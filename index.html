    How it works

    Rules

    1. Pass the device. The next player taps Ready when they have the phone.
    2. Type any five-letter dictionary word. Letters score like Scrabble.
    3. After you are ready, one or more letters from the last word may freeze in place.
    4. Between turns, open the shop to spend points on sabotages — freeze extra letters, hide the clock, force backwards typing, and more.
    5. Five turns each. A tie goes to sudden death.
